Privacy Policy for Formora - iOS

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Formora is designed to keep user-created content private and stored locally on the user's device.

Data Collection

Formora does not collect or transmit users' personal data or user-created content to the developer or to Formora-operated servers.

Formora does not require users to create an account.

Local Data Storage

Data entered or created in Formora, including checklists, answers, notes, and attached photos, is stored locally on the user's device.

If a user chooses to enter personal information, such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, or other personal information, that information remains part of the locally stored content. Formora does not transmit this content to the developer.

Users can delete their locally stored content directly within the app. Removing the app may also remove locally stored application data, subject to iOS backup and device settings.

Device Permissions

Formora may request access to certain device features only when required for functionality initiated by the user:

  • Camera - to take and attach photos to checklist items.
  • Photos - to select and attach photos from the user's photo library.
  • Contacts - to save contact information to the user's device when the user explicitly chooses to do so.
  • Notifications - to provide reminders configured by the user.
  • Files - to import and export Formora files and reports.

These permissions can be managed or revoked through iOS Settings.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Formora may offer optional subscriptions and in-app purchases through Apple's App Store. Payments and subscription transactions are processed by Apple.

Formora does not receive or store users' payment card or banking information.

Advertising

The iOS version of Formora does not display third-party advertising.

Data Sharing

Formora does not sell user-created content or personal information to third parties.

Because Formora does not transmit user-created content to Formora-operated servers, the developer does not maintain a server-side copy of that content to retain or delete.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Formora's functionality or data practices change. The current version will be made available through Formora's Privacy Policy link.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: formoraapp@gmail.com
